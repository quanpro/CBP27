# The 1% Planner 2027 · overview

_Public summary of the 2027 program (v3, 22 Oct 2026). Owner: Quân, Planning Director, Planning & Customer Service, Marico South East Asia. The full plan of record, with business figures and the budget, is kept outside this public repository (`content-private/plan_of_record_v3.md`, never committed)._

**What it is.** Ten courses in three priorities for about 20 planners and customer-service staff. Each course runs as a six-week cycle: learn online on Blink, prove it in a checkpoint on this Hub, apply it in an offline forum with the Planning Director, then use it on live S&OP work signed off by the manager. Tagline: *Better every week* (1% a week compounds to 1.68× in a year).

## Program decisions that shape the build

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Priorities | 1. AI enabling and data literacy · 2. Problem solving and communication · 3. Core planning: capacity, MPS, MRP, demand and the business, SLOB, fill rate |
| Scope | Ten courses in 2027 (codes A = analytics and AI, C = communication and business, P = planning core); the rest of the catalogue moves to 2028 |
| Order | AI and data first; reading the ML forecast sits inside P4, later in the year |
| Offline cadence | One offline session about every six weeks; Pitch Day (June) and the capstone War Room (December) replace that cycle's forum, so 9 offline sessions in total |
| Faculty | The Planning Director facilitates every offline session |
| Online learning | Videos on the company's Blink platform. Blink hosts MP4 files only: no quizzes and no completion tracking |
| Tests | Baseline (60 min, EN with a VI toggle) and per-course predict, checkpoint and recall on this Hub; graded and stored in a private Google Sheet through Google Apps Script |
| Gate to forum | Pass the course checkpoint at 80% (8 of 10); retake with the other form after 24 h; a third try after a 15-minute chat with the manager |
| Privacy | Individual scores visible only to the person, the Planning Director and HR L&D; managers see "passed / not yet"; team views only for groups of 3 or more, managers excluded |
| IDP | Goals run on the fiscal year (April–March) in the HR system: a January–March 2027 bridge objective, then FY28 goals (1 business impact, 2 capability, 1 application) |

## Roles

- Planning Director: facilitates all offline sessions, reviews all new content, runs the managers' Leadership Circle, owns the Hub backend.
- Managers: host the 30-minute Monday huddle, sign off field work within a week, sponsor team projects, sit the Pitch Day panel.
- HR L&D: Blink upload and enrolment, IDP set-up in the HR system, PIN issue and privacy, logistics, certification and graduation.
- IT: confirm Google Apps Script and GitHub Pages are reachable from company laptops; agree the Copilot guideline.
