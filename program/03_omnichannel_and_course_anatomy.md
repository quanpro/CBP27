# Omnichannel model and course anatomy

_Part of The 1% Planner 2027 plan of record (v3, 22 Oct 2026). Owner: Quân, Planning Director._

## 5. Omnichannel: each channel does one job

| Channel | Job | What happens | Time per member per cycle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blink (online, phone or laptop) | Know | 6–9 animated episodes of 4–7 min, English voice + Vietnamese subtitles, same cast (Linh, Anh Minh, the coach) | ~50 min |
| The 1% Planner Hub (GitHub page) | Prove | Predict quiz before the videos (ungraded) · checkpoint after (80% gate) · spaced recall day 2/7/30 · my progress · capstone | ~40 min |
| Offline forum (Quân) | Apply | 45' field show-and-tell from the last course · 15' most-missed checkpoint ideas · 100' case on our own data in teams · 30' commit to the field assignment | 4 h |
| Field (live S&OP work) | Do | The assignment at the S&OP step the course serves; manager signs off with the rubric within a week | ~3 h |
| Monday huddle (managers, 30') + monthly Case Clinic (45', Teams, from April) | Sustain | Questions not lectures; one real problem statement red-teamed per clinic | ~1 h |
| Big moments | Celebrate | Kick-off (Jan) · Pitch Day (Jun) · War Room + graduation (Dec) | in the forum slots |

Per member: about 9.5 h per 6-week cycle, about 86 h a year (≈ 7 h a month, ≈ 4.5% of working time). Managers add the Leadership Circle (1 h/month) and sign-offs.

### Course anatomy (6 weeks)
Week 1–2 Learn (Blink + Predict) → end of week 2–3 Checkpoint (80%, retake from the second form after 24 h) → Forum → weeks 4–6 Field + manager sign-off → show-and-tell opens the next forum. Spaced recall arrives on day 2, 7 and 30 after the forum.


## 6. Tests and the Hub

### The 1% Planner Hub
- Public GitHub Pages site (new repo `the-1pct-planner`), no company data in the repo. Learner logs in with employee code + a personal PIN issued by HR.
- Backend: Google Apps Script web app owned by Quân; questions, answer keys and results live only in a private Google Sheet shared with Quân and HR L&D. Questions are served after login with options shuffled; grading happens on the server; the page never receives an answer key.
- Names stay in the private roster tab; every other record uses the employee code.
- Fallback if Google is blocked on Marico laptops: same item banks in Microsoft Forms quizzes (company sign-in); the Hub keeps the front door and links out.

### Baseline (60 min, EN with VI toggle; same instrument re-run 29 Nov–10 Dec 2027)
- Part A · Self-rating (≈ 6 min): one skill per course (10) on the 5-step scale 0 Not yet · BA Basic · WK Working · FO Fully operational · LE Leading edge.
- Part B · Knowledge and application (≈ 40 min): 40 questions, 4 per course, mixing recall, calculation and judgment; answer options balanced in length.
- Part C · Mini case "Shampoo X before Tết" (≈ 14 min): 6 questions that cut across courses (problem statement, capacity, MPS, MRP, cash, communication).
- Output: course-readiness heatmap (team view only with n ≥ 3; managers excluded from shared views), person-level gap list for the IDP, calibration flags (self-rating vs score).

### Per course
- Predict: 3 ungraded questions before the videos (pretesting raises later recall).
- Checkpoint: two parallel forms of 10 questions; pass = 8/10 (80%); retake with the other form after 24 h; a third try after a 15-minute chat with the manager. Gate: pass before the forum.
- Spaced recall: 3 questions on day 2, 7 and 30 after the forum, drawn from the form not taken.
- Capstone: team submission (one-day answer, KPI before/after with evidence, value, lessons) and panel scoring (VPSC, Quân, HR, managers) on a 5-criterion rubric.
