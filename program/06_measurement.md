# Measurement

_Part of The 1% Planner 2027 plan of record (v3, 22 Oct 2026). Owner: Quân, Planning Director._

## 10. Measurement (Kirkpatrick)
- L1 Reaction: one-minute forum pulse, score ≥ +50.
- L2 Learning: checkpoint first-try pass rate ≥ 70%, all members pass every checkpoint by the forum; baseline → re-test +25% on the course average.
- L3 Behaviour: field sign-offs on time; one-page problem statement at S&OE/DSR/S&OP from Apr; action titles ≥ 90% of S&OP/DSR slides from Aug; 100% weekly Copilot users; ≥ 80% of overrides logged with a reason.
- L4 Results (per course): hours saved (A2, A3) · output reliability and changeover minutes (P1, P2) · line stops for materials and RPM cover (P3) · bias, lag-1 wMAPE, FVA (P4) · SLOB value and write-offs (P5) · MT fill rate and lost sales (P6) · project results at Pitch Day and the War Room.
