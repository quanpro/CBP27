## 7. IDP and goals (FY Apr–Mar, in the HR system)

- **Bridge, Jan–Mar 2027 (FY27 Q4):** one development objective added to the current IDP: baseline done; A2, A3 and A1 checkpoints passed; team project chartered by 16 Apr.
- **FY28 IDP (set in April 2027, reviewed at mid-year Oct 2027 and year-end Mar 2028):**
  1. One business-impact goal from the person's role KPI and team project (number, date, guard rail).
  2. Two capability goals from the person's two largest baseline gaps: target level by Mar 2028, evidence = checkpoints passed, field sign-offs, re-test score.
  3. One application goal (a behaviour others can see), e.g. problem statement at every S&OE/DSR/S&OP issue from April; action titles and executive summary on every pack from July.
- Each goal carries 70-20-10 actions (field / forum + coaching / Blink) and milestones from the Hub. HR L&D proposes the weighting in the performance review (options A weighted goal 15–20% · B gate for "exceeds" · C development only).
- Role examples (illustrative numbers, to be replaced by baseline actuals):

  - Supply / production planner: raise Line 3 output reliability from 85% to 92% by Dec 2027 without adding overtime.
  - Materials planner (RPM/MSO): zero line stops for materials from Q2, and cut RPM write-offs on phase-outs by 30% vs FY27.
  - Customer service: raise fill rate for my top 5 MT accounts from 87% to 93% by Dec 2027 within the current stock budget.
  - Manager: every member of my team has a signed-off charter by 16 Apr and passes ≥ 8 of 10 checkpoints; upward feedback ≥ 4/5 on coaching.
