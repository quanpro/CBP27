# Blink upload guide (for HR L&D)

_Part of The 1% Planner 2027 plan of record (v3, 22 Oct 2026)._ Blink hosts the videos only. Quizzes, checkpoints and progress live on the 1% Planner Hub, which is the proof of completion.

## Structure
- One playlist (or channel/section) per course, named `The 1% Planner · <code> <Course name>`, e.g. `The 1% Planner · A2 Data to Decisions`.
- Inside: the trailer first, then episodes in order. Title format: `<code> · Ep<NN> · <Episode title>` (e.g. `A2 · Ep01 · The Monday Copy-Paste`).
- Description of every video (EN, then VI):
  > Part of The 1% Planner, course <code> <name>. Before you watch: do the 3-question Predict on the Hub. After the last episode: take the checkpoint on the Hub (pass 8/10) before the forum on <date>. Hub: <URL>
- Thumbnail: course code in the family colour (A blue, C teal, P navy) + episode number + Marico logo.
