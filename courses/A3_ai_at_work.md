## The business problem
Microsoft 365 Copilot is available but used rarely or unsafely; people do not know what it is good for in planning, and some paste company data into public tools.

## After this course you can
1. Use AI inside the company guideline: Microsoft 365 Copilot with the Marico account; no sensitive data in prompts unless IT confirms; never paste company data into public tools  
   _Dùng AI đúng hướng dẫn công ty: Microsoft 365 Copilot với tài khoản Marico; không đưa dữ liệu nhạy cảm vào prompt khi IT chưa xác nhận; không dán dữ liệu công ty vào công cụ công cộng_
2. Write prompts with role, task, context and format  
   _Viết prompt có vai trò, nhiệm vụ, bối cảnh và định dạng_
3. Use Copilot in Outlook, Teams, Excel and PowerPoint for three or more own tasks  
   _Dùng Copilot trong Outlook, Teams, Excel và PowerPoint cho từ ba việc của mình_
4. Verify every number and fact AI produces against the source  
   _Kiểm chứng mọi con số và dữ kiện AI đưa ra với nguồn gốc_
5. Know when not to use AI: decisions, approvals, anything you cannot check  
   _Biết khi nào không dùng AI: quyết định, phê duyệt, điều bạn không kiểm chứng được_

Self-rating statement (baseline Part A): "I use Microsoft 365 Copilot safely every week to save time, and I check everything it produces."

## Episodes on Blink (English voice, Vietnamese subtitles, 4–7 min each)
Every episode follows the house format from A1/C1: cold open with a hook → 8-second commit → title sting → three chapters with one 20-second pause-and-try → lightning round (3 checks) → takeaway with a cliffhanger. Cast: Linh (junior planner who makes the mistakes), Anh Minh (manager), the coach (narrator). Illustrative Marico-style numbers only; no other companies' names or data.

| Ep | Title | Hook and key ideas | Pause-and-try |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | The Intern Who Never Sleeps | Cold open: Copilot writes a perfect customer email in 20 seconds, with one wrong number. What Copilot is and is not. | Find the wrong number. |
| 02 | The Guardrails | Our guideline: Microsoft 365 Copilot with the Marico account only; what never goes in a prompt; why public tools are out. | Allowed or not? Five fast cases. |
| 03 | Prompt Like a Planner | Role, task, context, format; before/after prompts for S&OE, DSR and Demand Review. | Rewrite a weak prompt. |
| 04 | Copilot Across Your Week | Outlook, Teams, Excel, PowerPoint: ten planner use cases from the team playbook. | Match the task to the app. |
| 05 | Trust, but Verify | Confident wrong answers; tracing numbers to the source; saying when AI drafted. | Check this summary against the table. |
| 06 | Where People Decide | Decisions, approvals and allocations stay with people; how this differs from the ML forecast (teaser for P4). | Human or AI? Sort six tasks. |
