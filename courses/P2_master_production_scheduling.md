## The business problem
Plans change late, lot sizes and campaigns are set by habit, Tết builds are improvised, and our MPS tool is not yet used to its potential.

## After this course you can
1. Explain MPS level, horizon and time fences (frozen, slushy, liquid)  
   _Giải thích cấp độ, tầm nhìn và các mốc thời gian của MPS (đóng băng, bán linh hoạt, linh hoạt)_
2. Set lot sizes and campaigns: MOQ, EOQ and the batch that respects both  
   _Xác định cỡ lô và chiến dịch sản xuất: MOQ, EOQ và cỡ lô thỏa cả hai_
3. Sequence to cut changeovers using the changeover matrix  
   _Sắp xếp trình tự để giảm chuyển đổi bằng ma trận chuyển đổi_
4. Plan seasonal prebuild (Tết) against capacity and cash  
   _Lập kế hoạch sản xuất trước mùa vụ (Tết) theo năng lực và dòng tiền_
5. Protect plan stability and read output reliability in both directions  
   _Giữ kế hoạch ổn định và đọc độ tin cậy sản lượng theo cả hai chiều_

Self-rating statement (baseline Part A): "I build a master production schedule that respects capacity, lot sizes and time fences, and I plan the Tết build without wasting cash."

## Episodes on Blink (English voice, Vietnamese subtitles, 4–7 min each)
Every episode follows the house format from A1/C1: cold open with a hook → 8-second commit → title sting → three chapters with one 20-second pause-and-try → lightning round (3 checks) → takeaway with a cliffhanger. Cast: Linh (junior planner who makes the mistakes), Anh Minh (manager), the coach (narrator). Illustrative Marico-style numbers only; no other companies' names or data.

| Ep | Title | Hook and key ideas | Pause-and-try |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | The Plan the Factory Can Keep | Cold open: Monday's plan is dead by Wednesday. What an MPS is: level, horizon, release. | Which level should this decision sit at? |
| 02 | Frozen, Slushy, Liquid | Time fences and the exception rule; cost of breaking the frozen zone. | Allowed in which zone? |
| 03 | How Big Is a Batch? | MOQ, EOQ, campaigns; the batch that respects both. | Size the campaign. |
| 04 | The Sequence Game | Changeover matrix (links P1); size first, then light to dark. | Find the cheaper sequence. |
| 05 | Building for Tết | Prebuild against capacity and cash; phase it late. | How short are we after prebuild? |
| 06 | When One Line Is Not Enough | Second lines and co-packers; costed options for overload. | Rank three options by cost. |
| 07 | Reliability Both Ways | Output reliability under and over; plan stability; the weekly meeting; our MPS tool. | Is 115% good? |
