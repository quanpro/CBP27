## 2. Part 1 form rule (random now, swap at re-test)

- Config: `p1_forms = A,B,C`, `p1_live = B,C` (set after the pilot; any two), `p1_backup = A`.
- First wave: serve the live form with fewer starts so far **within the person's `role_area`** in this wave (balance by role, so no role group takes mostly one form); tie → hash(employee_code + wave). Never serve the backup automatically.
- Recommended set-up after the equivalence review (31 Oct 2026): `p1_live = B,C`, `p1_backup = A` (predicted means A 18.7, B 18.4, C 18.2 of 32; Form A drafts were circulated in earlier reviews, and Form A keeps two hedged keys and one internal leak that B and C no longer have).
- Re-test wave (`2027-12`): serve the live form the person did **not** take in the previous wave. If they took the backup, serve the live form with fewer starts.
- Backup: served only when an admin adds a row to `Overrides` (`employee_code, wave, part, form, reason, by, at`) — used for a leak, a re-sit after a technical failure, or a person who saw a live form.
- Sections are timed separately: Section A locks at 45:00 (auto-advance), then Section B opens with its own 20:00. No going back from B to A. Server grace 5 min per section.
- Unanswered = wrong; there is no negative marking, so the intro tells people to answer every question.
- Exhibits: Section A items carry `exhibit_ids`; the exhibit panel stays visible (sticky on desktop, collapsible on phone), as in v1 Part C.

**Scoring:** raw = correct ÷ 32. Also store `by_step` (Quân's 7 steps), `by_move` (ASK, SHAPE, SIZE, CHECK, SO WHAT), `section_A_pct`, `section_B_pct`.
**Equating:** after the pilot and again after the wave, `buildSummary()` computes each form's mean and SD. If live-form means differ by more than 5 points, report a mean-sigma linear-equated score on Form A's scale: `scaled = mean_A + sd_A × (raw − mean_X) ÷ sd_X`. Store both `raw_pct` and `scaled_pct`; reports use `scaled_pct`. (With ~20 people this is a rough correction: also keep the pilot design of 2–3 people per form.)

## 3. Part 2 · MAP behaviours

- **2A SJT**: item_type `sjt`. Each item has 4 options; the learner picks one MOST and one LEAST (they cannot be the same option). Score per item: +1 if MOST = `answer_most`, +1 if LEAST = `answer_least` (0–2). Part 2A score = points ÷ 30, and by charter (3 items each = 6 points).
- **Order is fixed: 2A first, then 2B.** 2A is submitted and locked before 2B opens (the 2B statements describe the behaviours the 2A keys reward, so showing them first would leak answers). No going back to 2A.
- **2B self-rating**: item_type `rating`, 23 statements, scale 1 Rarely · 2 Sometimes · 3 Often · 4 Almost always, each with its 1 and 4 behavioural anchors shown under the buttons. All required.
- **Charter index** (admin view) = 0.3 × SJT% + 0.7 × manager score (rescaled 0–100); weights in Config (`p2_w_sjt = 0.3`, `p2_w_mgr = 0.7`) so Quân can change them.
- **Pilot only (optional)**: after choosing MOST/LEAST, the pilot group also rates each option 1–4 for effectiveness (item_type `sjt_rate`, stored in Ratings). Used to compare reliability against the MOST/LEAST format before the November wave; not scored.
- **Manager rating (180°)**: new page `rate.html` (role `manager` or `admin`). Rater ↔ ratee comes from `Roster.manager_code`; Quân is the `manager_code` of the 3 managers and of his direct reports. Same 23 statements in third person, plus "N/A – not observed". Opens with the window, closes `rating_close` (default 4 Dec). The rater sees the rater guide first (one page, in the item bank). One submission per rater–ratee–wave; editable until close.
- The learner never sees their manager's individual ratings item by item. They see, per charter: SJT %, own mean, manager mean, and the flags below, but only after the rating window closes.
- Flags per charter: self − manager ≥ 1.0 → "blind spot"; manager − self ≥ 1.0 → "hidden strength". Top 2 development behaviours = the two statements with the lowest manager score (tie → largest self − manager gap).

## 4. Part 3 · Core planning

- Module by `Roster.role_area`: demand → DP, supply → SP, materials → RPM, cs → CS. New optional column `Roster.p3_module` overrides it (for managers or mixed roles).
- Serve: 10 CORE items, then 18 module items; order fixed by item id within each block (options shuffled per attempt as today). One timer of 45 min, grace 5 min.
- KPI glossary panel (from `meta.glossary`) visible throughout.
- Scoring: total % (of 28), `core_pct`, `module_pct`, `by_level` (BA, WK, FO, LE), `by_gate`. Indicative CCBT level per person = the highest level at which they score ≥ 70% of that level's items, provided all lower levels are also ≥ 70% (cut scores are reset from the pilot).

## 5. Result screen (no item feedback in any part, so the re-test stays valid)

- After each sitting: "Part X done", time used, and nothing else, until all three parts are done.
- After all three parts + the close of the rating window: one profile page. It shows Part 1 by step and by move; Part 2 by charter (SJT, self, manager, flags); Part 3 by level and gate; the course self-ratings next to the course scores; the two largest gaps for the IDP ("Take these two to your IDP conversation in December").
- Admin (Sheet): heatmap by person × skill, calibration flags, form balance, item statistics (p-value, point-biserial, distractor %), and rater statistics (mean, spread by rater, to spot leniency).
