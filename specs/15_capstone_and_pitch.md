# 15 · Pitch Day and capstone

Two stages use the same submission form and the same rubric.

| Stage | When | What teams bring | Panel | Weighting |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pitch Day | Tue 22 Jun 2027 (C1 offline session) | Project charter + mid-point results, ≤ 8 slides with action titles | VPSC, Quân, HR L&D, the three managers | Communication counts double |
| Capstone · S&OP War Room | Tue 21 Dec 2027 (full day) | Verified results, value, the rule or process that keeps it, ≤ 10 slides; then a mock S&OP cycle run by the whole team | Same panel | Result and value count double |

## Submission (capstone.html → Capstone tab)
Project title · team members (codes) · sponsor manager · one-day answer as SOI (Situation ≤ 60 words, Observation ≤ 60 words ending in the question, Implication ≤ 60 words) · KPI name, before, after, unit · evidence link (SharePoint, internal only) · value in VND and how it was calculated (agreed with Finance for the capstone) · lessons · stage.

## Rubric (5 criteria × 1–5; anchors in `templates/capstone_rubric.md`)
1. Problem defined: a gap in numbers, a date, a decision-maker, guard rails.
2. Structured analysis: MECE tree, priorities, hypotheses tested with data.
3. Verified result: KPI before/after from the system of record, sustained ≥ 4 weeks.
4. Value and sustainability: VND basis agreed; the rule, process or tool change that keeps it.
5. Communication: answer first, action titles, one-page summary, handles questions.

Team score = average of judges; stage weights applied; certificates at graduation:
- 1% Practitioner: all checkpoints passed + all field sign-offs.
- 1% Expert: Practitioner + project scores ≥ 3.5 at capstone with a verified result + fully operational (FO) in own role at re-test.
- 1% Coach: Expert + co-teaches a forum segment, coaches a peer and reaches leading edge (LE) in one skill.
