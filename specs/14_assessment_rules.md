# 14 · Assessment rules

## Baseline (wave 2026-11; re-test wave 2027-12 with the same items)
- Window: 16–27 Nov 2026 (re-test 29 Nov–10 Dec 2027). One attempt per wave; resume allowed; 60-minute timer, server grace 10 minutes.
- Part A: 10 self-ratings (one per course), scale 0 Not yet · 1 Basic (BA) · 2 Working knowledge (WK) · 3 Fully operational (FO) · 4 Leading edge (LE). All 10 required to submit.
- Part B: 40 items (4 per course). Part C: mini case "Shampoo X before Tết", 6 items scored to P1, P2, A1, P4, C1, P3.
- Scores: overall % and % per course (Part B + case). No item feedback, no answer key shown.
- Result screen: your course readiness bars, your self-rating next to your score per course, and "your two largest gaps" (lowest course scores; tie → lower self-rating first) with the sentence "Take these two to your IDP conversation in December."
- Calibration flag (admin view only): self-rating ≥ 3 and course score < 50% → "rates high, scores low"; self-rating ≤ 1 and course score ≥ 80% → "rates low, scores high".

## Predict (per course)
- 3 items, ungraded, unlimited attempts; shown before the videos.
- After submit: the right answers with one-line "why" — the point is to prime attention (pretesting effect).

## Checkpoint (per course) = the gate to the offline forum
- Two parallel forms (A, B) of 10 items; same objective blueprint (see each course page).
- First attempt: form A if the learner's Roster row number is odd, B if even (balances the forms). Later attempts alternate.
- Pass mark 80% (8/10). Unanswered = wrong.
- Retake: allowed 24 h after the previous submission, with the other form.
- Third and later attempts: need an Unlocks row (the manager or Quân records a 15-minute coaching chat).
- Deadline: the course's `checkpoint_close` date (Friday before the forum). Learners who have not passed by then still attend the forum but finish the checkpoint in the following week with the manager; the gate shows on the manager's `team_status`.
- Result screen when passed: score, objectives all ✓ or which one to revisit, the missed items with the right answer and why, the forum date.
- Result screen when not yet: score, the objectives to revisit (named), the missed items with right answer and why, the time the retake opens, and the Blink episode to rewatch for each weak objective (episode map in each course page).

## Spaced recall
- After a pass, Recall rows are created for forum date + 2, + 7, + 30 days.
- 3 items each, from the form the learner did not take (if both taken, the form with fewer correct answers). Ungraded for the gate; score stored for L2 measurement; review shown after submit.

## Item hygiene (already applied to the bank; keep it when adding items)
- Four options, one correct, written so the correct option is not systematically the longest (bank today: correct option uniquely longest in 26% of items, chance = 25%; never more than 15% longer than the longest distractor).
- Answer letters shuffled per item; options shuffled again per attempt by the server.
- Illustrative Marico-style numbers; no other companies' names or data; no real customer names.
- Every item maps to one course objective; every objective has at least one item per checkpoint form.

## Scoring summaries
- Team view: average baseline % per course and average self-rating per course, by role group (n ≥ 3), managers excluded.
- Program KPIs (L2): checkpoint first-try pass rate per course (target ≥ 70%); % passed by forum date (target 100% within one week after the forum); baseline → re-test change per course (target +25% on the course average).
