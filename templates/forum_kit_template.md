# Offline forum kit · template (4 hours, Vietnamese, facilitated by Quân)

Slot: Tuesday afternoon of S&OP week 4 (after S&OP sign-off, before BAM and month-end), 13:30–17:30. Room for 22 people in teams of 4–5, mixed roles (demand, supply, materials, CS). Managers sit with their teams as hosts, not as judges.

## Before the forum
- T−7 days: checkpoint closes (Friday). Pull `Dash_Gate`: who has not passed (send them to their manager), the three most-missed items.
- T−5 days: prepare the case data pack (our own data, anonymised where needed) and print team packs.
- T−2 days: send the field-assignment card template; remind the previous course's teams to bring their field work.

## Run-sheet
| Time | Block | What happens | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Open | Why this course matters this month: one KPI, one number from our business | Purpose on the wall |
| 0:10–0:55 | Show-and-tell (previous course) | Each team shows one field result in 8 minutes: before/after, what they would do differently; one manager comment per team | Signed-off field work; 1% Award nominee |
| 0:55–1:10 | Most-missed ideas | The three ideas the checkpoint shows we still get wrong; the common wrong answer and why it is tempting | Misconceptions named |
| 1:10–1:20 | Break | | |
| 1:20–3:00 | Case on our data | Brief (10′) → team work (60′, coach rounds) → 3-minute answer-first presentations (20′) → debrief with the one-day answer (10′) | One-day answer per team |
| 3:00–3:30 | Commit | Each person writes a field-assignment card: what, at which S&OP step, by when, who signs off; managers countersign | Field cards (photo into Teams) |
| 3:30–3:45 | Close | 1% Award; one-minute pulse (NPS, usefulness, one thing I will use, one thing to change) | Pulse score |

## After the forum
- Within 48 h: photos of cards to each manager; recall rows created; slides and case answers on Teams.
- Week +1: managers' Monday huddle checks field progress (questions, not lectures).
- Week +3: field sign-off with the rubric; results go to the next forum's show-and-tell.

## Facilitator rules
- Questions before answers; let teams struggle for a few minutes before helping (productive failure).
- No leaderboards; praise teams publicly, correct individuals privately.
- Every case ends with a decision someone could act on Monday.
