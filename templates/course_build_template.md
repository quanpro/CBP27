# Course build template (for the 7 courses still to build: A2, A3, P2, P3, P4, P5, P6)

Each course is built one cycle ahead of its online window and reviewed by Quân before upload. Follow the house style proven in A1 and C1 (project docs `module-A1/A1_video_scripts_EN_VN.md`, `module-C1/C1_video_scripts_EN_VN.md`).

## 1 · Course brief (1 page)
Business problem in our numbers · the S&OP step where it is used · 4–6 objectives (already fixed in `courses/<code>.md`; the checkpoint items test exactly these) · the misconception we will attack first · the field assignment and its evidence · the KPI it moves.

## 2 · Episode plan (6–9 episodes, 4–7 min each)
For each episode: title (a hook, not a topic) · cold open (≤ 30 s, a mistake Linh makes or a surprising number) · the 8-second commit question · three chapters · one 20-second pause-and-try · a lightning round of 3 checks (must map to the course objectives) · takeaway line · cliffhanger into the next episode. The episode titles in `courses/<code>.md` are the starting point.

## 3 · Script (EN voice + VN subtitle line under each sentence)
- Cast: Coach (narrator), Linh (junior planner who voices the mistakes), Anh Minh (manager). CEO/VPSC appear only as a voice in cold opens.
- Conversational, short sentences; numbers said the way people say them.
- Illustrative Marico-style numbers (Shampoo X, Line 3, MT/GT, Tết). No other companies' names or data, no real customer names, no internal program names.
- Misconception first, then the idea, then a worked example on our kind of data, then the pause-and-try.

## 4 · Production rules (as approved for A1/C1, Oct 2026)
- v4 motion style (bean characters with expressions, continuous camera moves, kinetic typography, one visual metaphor per idea); music style C (upbeat pop) at the agreed level under the voice.
- English voice, Vietnamese subtitles burned in; one MP4 per episode, 1080p30, chapter markers; trailer ≤ 70 s.
- File name: `<code>_EpNN_<Title_With_Underscores>.mp4` (e.g. `A2_Ep01_The_Monday_Copy_Paste.mp4`).

## 5 · Forum kit
Case on our own data (anonymised where needed), team pack, facilitator notes with the one-day answer, field-assignment card, using `templates/forum_kit_template.md`.

## 6 · Assessment
Predict (3) and checkpoint forms A and B (10 each) already exist in `content-private/`. After the episodes are final, check every checkpoint item against the episodes: each item's idea must be taught; if not, change the episode, not the item, unless the item is wrong.
