# Field-work sign-off rubric (manager, within one week)

Signed off when the work scores 2 or more on all four criteria. A 1 on any criterion = one more attempt within two weeks.

| Criterion | 1 · Not yet | 2 · Meets | 3 · Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Applied to real work | Exercise on sample data only | Done on own plan, order or SKU at the named S&OP step | Changed a real decision or number |
| Correct method | Steps missing or wrong | Method applied correctly | Method adapted sensibly to the case |
| Evidence | No numbers | Before and after numbers shown | Impact quantified in units or VND |
| Explained | Cannot explain why | Explains the why in 2 minutes | Teaches it to a peer |

How: the learner posts the work and a 2-minute explanation in the team channel; the manager scores within one week and writes one sentence of feedback. HR sees only "signed off: yes/no" per person and course.
