# Pitch Day and capstone rubric (each judge scores 1–5 per criterion)

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Problem defined | A topic, no numbers | Gap in numbers and a date | Gap, date, decision-maker and guard rails; passes all six characteristics |
| 2 · Structured analysis | A list of causes | A MECE tree with priorities | Hypothesis-driven tree, 80:20 focus, hypotheses tested with data, iterated |
| 3 · Verified result | No measured change | KPI moved, from the system of record | KPI moved and held ≥ 4 weeks, with a clean before/after and no side effects |
| 4 · Value and sustainability | No value | Value in VND estimated | Value agreed with Finance; a rule, process or tool change keeps it |
| 5 · Communication | Bottom-up, no clear ask | Answer first, mostly action titles | One-page summary, action title on every slide, handles questions crisply |

Weights: Pitch Day — criterion 5 counts double. Capstone — criteria 3 and 4 count double. Team score = average of judges (weighted). Comments: one thing to keep, one thing to change.
